### Audit 8 — Independent second-pass research

Not yet executed. The order requires it after the reconstruction and the first seven audits. Under the Norton scope it will re-check, with sources independent of those the records cite, the transcription of both tables of contents, the date of first publication of every selection (the provisional dates in the lists included) and each record's attribution and bibliography.

## Method

**Scope.** 1 January 1946 to 31 December 2000 (decision of 2026-11-06). The core window is 1951–2000, and 1946–1950 is reported separately as the bridge from installment 9.
- **Coverage (third scope decision of 2026-11-06, which replaces the first two):** the authors and works of the Norton anthologies. British, Irish and Commonwealth writing follows *The Norton Anthology of English Literature*, 11th edition (2024), volume F (*The Twentieth and Twenty-First Centuries*). American writing follows *The Norton Anthology of American Literature*, volume E (*1945 to the Present*), in the selection of the 11th edition, whose table of contents Norton has published ahead of the edition (announced for fall 2027): no table of contents of the 10th edition (2022), the one in print, could be reached. Every selection first published 1946–2000 gets a record of its own, one per poem, story, essay or play, and an excerpt is recorded as the book it comes from. Authors with no such selection are not covered. Works already recorded for a Norton author beyond the selections (Larkin's, Orwell's and Selvon's books) are kept; the records of the eleven authors whom Norton does not anthologise were removed (section 13).
- **Inclusion test:** literary activity and publication in the period, not birth date or fame.

**Records.**
- **Works.** Every literary work gets its own work record, with depth tiered by significance:
  - *major:* the full seven-part record, with a plot or argument of several paragraphs;
  - *significant:* every analytical field, shorter;
  - *minor:* shorter but specific.
- **Seven-part mapping:**
  - overview → `description`
  - content → `summary`
  - summary or argument → `plot_or_argument`
  - themes → `thematic_analysis`
  - literary characteristics → `narrative_structure` and `meter_and_prosody`
  - significance → `literary_significance`
  - critical context → `critical_context`
- **Bibliographic and verification fields:**
  - `bibliographical_notes` holds the first-edition details.
  - `research_notes` holds unresolved bibliographical questions.
  - `verification_status` records what has actually been checked.
- **Authors** carry a `bibliography_status` (complete, substantially complete or incomplete) and a written statement of what the bibliography covers, how it was checked and what is still missing.
- **Shared authors.** A writer recorded by an earlier installment keeps that single author record; installment-10 works link to it.

**Strict build.** `META["strict"]` turns off all template enrichment. `scripts/strict_checks.py` blocks the build when:
- a field falls short of its depth tier;
- boilerplate appears;
- a sentence is repeated across works;
- a date falls outside 1946–2000;
- a taxonomy name is undefined;
- a record is marked verified without the sources actually consulted.

No source is attached to records wholesale. Installments 8–9 attached four general reference works to every record; installment 10 does not.

**Baseline.** The two tables of contents are transcribed selection by selection into `research/installment_10/lists/norton_nael_11e_vol_f.csv` (288 selections, with Norton's page numbers and the authors' dates it prints) and `research/installment_10/lists/norton_naal_11e_vol_e.csv` (218 selections; the brochure prints neither, but marks the selections new to the 11th edition). Each row gives the year of first publication, the basis of that date, the scope it implies (1946–2000; before 1946; after 2000; written before 1946 but first published later, excluded; or still to be dated) and the title the record has or will have. A poem counts from its first printing, in a periodical or a book. Most dates are provisional, from general knowledge, and are checked as each record is built; the dates that decided whether an author is covered at all were checked now (section 14). Audit 1 measures coverage against these rows, and the coverage matrix lists every work record and every selection still to record or to date.

**Superseded research.** Under the first two scope decisions the baseline was the discovery inventory (`research/installment_10/discovery_inventory.csv`, built by `scripts/i10_discovery.py`): 6,369 candidate authors from the peer-reviewed datasets of the Post45 Data Collective and ten prize and series lists transcribed into `research/installment_10/lists/`. It stays in the repository as research, with its lists and their source records, frozen as of wave 12 (commit `8264c2d`): its status column still counts the removed authors as recorded. It no longer defines the installment.

**Verification levels.**
- *verified:* dates and bibliography cross-checked in at least two independent sources, listed with the record.
- *partially verified:* bibliographic facts checked, but the summary or critical text written from knowledge of the work and not checked against a source in this pass.
- *unverified:* not checked.
